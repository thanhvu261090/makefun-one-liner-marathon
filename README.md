## The entry

> My smart fridge just emailed me a grocery list, a therapy referral, and a restraining order against the midnight snacker — which, tragically, is me.

## Why it's funny

The joke takes the "smart home" promise literally and escalates it in three
beats: a helpful grocery list, an unexpectedly personal therapy referral, and
finally a legal order — against the owner himself. The fridge isn't just smart,
it's fed up, organised, and one step ahead of its own midnight raider. The
punchline lands because the villain, the victim, and the snacker are all the
same person standing in front of the fridge at 2 a.m.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This writes the single entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.
